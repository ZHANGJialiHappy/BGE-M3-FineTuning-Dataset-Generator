### Channel List (See Drawing 6655-0145)

The channel list screen contains status information of all alarm channels within the ECS, no matter the status of the individual alarm channel. As default, the alarm channels are listed in tag-name alphabetic 
order. From the channel list screen, it is possible to cut out (and re-activate) alarm channels.
